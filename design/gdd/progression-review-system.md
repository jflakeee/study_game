## Overview
Sources: mastery.ts, review.ts, review-v2.ts, learning.ts, main.ts.
## Detailed Design
Mastery rebuilt from v1 attempt history. Concept prerequisite metadata exists but does not gate grade selection. Review uses again/hard/good for wrong/hinted/independent. v2 response-time helper is not wired into runtime.
## Formulas
Mastery wrong=max(0,L-1); hinted=max(L,2); independent=max(L,3) or max(L,4) after >=2 distinct independent correct IDs. Level 5 unreachable. Independent count increments even for repeated IDs, distinct list deduped.
Review: again interval=1, repetitions=0; otherwise reps=previous+1. hard=max(1,round(prev×1.2) or 1); good=prev?max(prev+1,round(prev×2.2)):1; easy=prev?max(prev+2,round(prev×3)):3. Due=now+interval×86400000 ms. v2 independent <=7000ms easy, >=45000ms hard, otherwise good; hinted hard and wrong again take precedence.
## Edge Cases
Not FSRS; no max interval; malformed dates and extreme intervals need validation. INTENT UNKNOWN: level 5, timing fairness, curriculum prerequisites, interval caps. Do not activate v2 without policy review.
## Dependencies
Attempt history, concepts, question IDs, clock and review persistence.
## Acceptance Criteria
Regression: distinct IDs for level4, replay v1 history, wrong decrement, hint floor, due date arithmetic, no timing requirement in current UI.
