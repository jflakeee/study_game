## Detailed Design
First wrong gives hint; second gives generic alternative-thinking prompt; third gives explanation and retest message. Correct ends session. Explicit hint sets usedHint; wrong attempts mark hint use. Third wrong saves incorrect attempt and UI finishes, despite tutor retest state. No guaranteed similar-question selection.
